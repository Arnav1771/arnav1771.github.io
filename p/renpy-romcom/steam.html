About This Game

On an ordinary morning, your instinct saves a stranger's life — and that single second reshapes everything. The next day, you receive a letter of special admission to Celestia Academy, an elite school where you don't belong, where everyone else was born into it, and where the person you saved is watching to see who you really are.

The Third Eye is a grounded, emotional romance visual novel with a gender-selectable protagonist, six fully-realized love interests, and a affection system that genuinely shapes which of several stories you end up living.

  • Six Love Interests, Two Full Route Sets. Play as a male or female protagonist, each opening a distinct trio of romanceable characters.
  • An Affection System With Teeth. Every scene nudges a relationship up or down — reach the threshold and a route locks in, fall short and it quietly closes.
  • The Third Eye Mechanic. A Chapter 5 turning point lets you fight for your own romance or step back and witness someone else's story unfold instead.
  • A Living Matchmaker Subplot. Push side characters toward each other and watch a second love story develop in the background of your own.
  • Harem & Queen's Court Endings. Sustain multiple relationships at once to unlock polyamorous multi-partner endings.
  • Eight Full Chapters. A complete prologue-to-ending story on every route, with multiple ending variants based on your choices.
