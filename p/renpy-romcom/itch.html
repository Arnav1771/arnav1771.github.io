The Third Eye

A romance visual novel about the person you save, the school you don't belong in, and six stories you can choose to live — or just witness.

A visual novel by Arnav1771 · Ren'Py · Visual Novel, Romance, Dating Sim

The Story

On an ordinary morning, you save a stranger's life without thinking twice. The next day, a letter of special admission arrives to Celestia Academy — a school built for people who were born into it. You weren't.

Over a prologue and eight chapters, you navigate a school where every friendship is a negotiation and every kindness has a cost. Choose your protagonist's gender, build relationships with six distinct love interests, and reach a Chapter 5 turning point — the "Third Eye" — where you decide whether to fight for your own story or step back and let someone else's unfold.

Features

  • Gender-selectable protagonist with two full sets of routes
  • Six love interests, each with an independent affection system and route
  • The Third Eye mechanic — a mid-game choice that can redirect your entire route
  • A background matchmaker subplot between side characters
  • Harem and Queen's Court multi-partner endings
  • Multiple endings per route based on affection and late-game choices

Content & Warnings

Family-friendly romance — no explicit content. The prologue includes a non-graphic traffic-accident scene (played for heroism, not injury detail). Character designs and tone are shonen/shoujo-style throughout.

Credits

Written and built with Ren'Py. Full technical documentation and source available on GitHub.
