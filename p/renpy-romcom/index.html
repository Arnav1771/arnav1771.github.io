The Hook

A single second of instinct rewrites everything.

On an ordinary morning, you save a stranger's life without thinking twice. The next day, an invitation arrives to Celestia Academy — a school built for people who were born into it. You weren't.

Over a prologue and eight chapters, you navigate a school where every friendship is a negotiation and every kindness has a cost. The person you saved is watching. So is everyone else. What you do next decides whose story you become part of — and whose you're only allowed to witness.

How You Play

Every choice has a person's name on it.

The Third Eye tracks affection, not points. Four systems shape which of six stories you end up living.

Gender Selection

Play as a male or female protagonist. Your choice opens a distinct set of routes and reshapes how the academy sees you.

Affection System

Every scene nudges a relationship up or down. Reach twenty and a route locks in — fall short, and it quietly closes.

The Third Eye

At the Chapter 5 turning point, learn a hard truth about your rival — then choose to fight for your own story, or step back and watch someone else's unfold.

Matchmaker

Push two side characters toward each other — Shroud and Lune, Miguel and Rosty — and watch a second love story unfold in the background of your own.

The Cast

Six people. Six ways to be seen.

Every love interest has their own affection arc, their own route, and their own ending — good, neutral, or bad.

Keqing

The chairman's son — composed on the surface, still learning how to accept being saved.

Lazy by daylight, a different person entirely once the stream goes live at night.
